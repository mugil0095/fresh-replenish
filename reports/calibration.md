# Calibrated forecast ranges

Each model's deciles were shifted per product category with split-conformal calibration: a one-sided shift per decile, learned on Phase 4's 137,984 train-period fold forecasts per model and scaled by each forecast's own spread (q90 - q10). The check is the held-out week (2024-06-26 to 2024-07-02), which played no part in training or calibration: 4,928 series in 90 stores, forecast from all earlier days, with demand recovered as in Phase 3. Generated by `uv run python -m fresh_replenish.uncertainty.conformal`.

## Headline

- **Exit check:** after calibration, the 10-90% ranges cover 78.7% to 80.0% of held-out demand (target 80% +/- 2 points). Within the target: lightgbm, chronos2, chronos2 without inputs, timesfm2.5, toto2; outside it: none.

- **Before calibration** they covered 74.9% to 78.5%.

- **Held-out accuracy:** lightgbm has the lowest WAPE (32.5%); seasonal naive has 41.7%.
