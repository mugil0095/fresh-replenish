# What drives daily demand

Poisson regression (PPML) of daily sales on the train split (90 days), with store-product and week fixed effects and standard errors clustered by store. Generated by `uv run python -m fresh_replenish.analysis.drivers`.

**These are associations, not causes.** Discounts and promotions are not assigned at random, and sales on stockout days understate demand (Phase 3 recovers it).

## Headline

- **10% price cut:** +14.2% sales (95% CI +13.8% to +14.6%).

- **promotion running:** +1.4% sales (95% CI +0.8% to +2.0%).

- **non-working day:** +31.7% sales (95% CI +30.9% to +32.5%).

- **Stability:** 7 of 7 drivers keep their sign and size across two random halves of the stores.
