# Recovering demand hidden by stockouts

Using the dataset paper's own masking, we hid 12.7% of hours in a random sample of 90 stores (4,928 store-product series). We then scored daily totals on the 76,684 days that had hidden hours but no real stockout, where the true demand is known. WAPE is the average error as a share of demand; WPE is the bias, and negative means demand is understated. Intervals come from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of series. Generated by `uv run python -m fresh_replenish.imputation.recovery`.

## Headline

- **Without recovery,** demand on those days is understated by 70.9%.

- **Best method: profile.** WAPE 37.9% (95% CI 37.2% to 38.6%); bias -0.0% (95% CI -0.4% to +0.4%).

- **Used for Phase 4:** profile (lowest WAPE). Recovered demand for all series is in `data/processed/recovered_demand.parquet`.
