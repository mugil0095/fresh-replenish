# Cost-based orders

Each policy orders for every series and day of the held-out week. The data has no prices, so a wasted unit costs 1 and a lost sale costs r; unsold stock is thrown away at the end of the day and missed sales are not made up later. Demand is recovered as in Phase 3. Cost is per unit of demand. Intervals come from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of series, the same resamples for every policy, so differences are paired. Seasonal naive orders last week's same-day sales; lightgbm median orders the most accurate model's median; every other policy orders that model's calibrated forecast at the critical ratio r / (1 + r), the newsvendor rule. r = 9 is the headline because quick commerce now runs availability-first (see `docs/decisions.md`). Generated by `uv run python -m fresh_replenish.decisions.newsvendor`.
