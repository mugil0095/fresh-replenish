# Forecasting daily demand 7 days ahead

Each model forecast the next 7 days of recovered demand (Phase 3) from 4 weekly origins in the train period, for 4,928 store-product series in a random 90 stores: 137,984 forecast days per model. The foundation models are zero-shot; LightGBM is trained on each fold's history. WAPE is the average error as a share of demand; WPE is the bias (negative means too low); MASE compares the error with repeating last week; WQL is the scaled pinball loss of the deciles; coverage_80 is the share of actual demand inside the 10-90% range (target 80%, calibrated in Phase 5). Intervals come from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of series, and the same resamples pair the models. Generated by `uv run python -m fresh_replenish.forecasting.evaluate`.

## Headline

- **Best model: lightgbm.** WAPE 33.2% (95% CI 32.4% to 34.0%); bias -9.4% (95% CI -9.9% to -8.9%).

- **Against seasonal naive** (WAPE 44.2%): beat it in every fold: lightgbm, chronos2, chronos2 without inputs, timesfm2.5, toto2; do not: none.

- **Against LightGBM** (paired WAPE gap, negative is better): chronos2 +1.0% (95% CI +0.8% to +1.2%); chronos2 without inputs +1.6% (95% CI +1.4% to +1.8%); timesfm2.5 +1.3% (95% CI +1.1% to +1.5%); toto2 +1.9% (95% CI +1.7% to +2.1%).
